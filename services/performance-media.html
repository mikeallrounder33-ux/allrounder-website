Performance Media

Paid built and paced against a target cost per outcome — not against impressions, reach, or whatever number happens to look best this month.

EngagementMonthly, ongoing
Best forD2C, e-commerce, lead gen
Pairs withContent & Creative

What it is

The build and day-to-day running of paid channels, against a cost-per-outcome target agreed up front. Account structure, tracking, creative testing, budget pacing and the weekly decisions that decide whether spend compounds or leaks.

The unglamorous half of this work is measurement. Most underperforming accounts we inherit are not badly optimised — they are optimising toward a conversion event that doesn't reflect what the business actually earns. We fix that first.

Channels we run

  • Meta — Facebook and Instagram, prospecting through retention
  • Google — Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube
  • TikTok — where the audience and the creative can support it
  • LinkedIn — for B2B with a considered sales cycle
  • Retail & marketplace search — Amazon and equivalent

We won't recommend a channel because it's fashionable. If your audience isn't reachable there at a sane cost, it doesn't go in the plan.

What's included

Account audit or build

Structure, naming, audiences and exclusions — from scratch or fixed in place.

Tracking & conversion setup

Server-side where it matters, so the numbers you optimise toward are real.

Creative testing

A structured queue of variants, with a rule for when a test is called.

Budget pacing

Weekly reallocation across channels against the cost-per-outcome target.

Landing page feedback

What the traffic hits, and what's costing you conversion once it lands.

Reporting

Weekly numbers, a monthly written read, and the decision each one leads to.

How it runs

  1. Audit. Existing accounts, tracking accuracy and what the current data can and can't tell us.
  2. Fix measurement. Conversion events, values and attribution settings, before spend changes.
  3. Rebuild. Campaign structure against the plan, with a clear testing framework.
  4. Launch and pace. Ramp deliberately; hold budget until the signal is real.
  5. Weekly cycle. Read, reallocate, queue the next creative test, report.

Common questions

Is there a minimum spend?

No hard minimum, but below a certain level the channels can't gather enough signal to optimise, and you're paying management fees on a budget too small to learn from. We'll tell you honestly if that's where you are — often the better move is to fix the offer or the creative first.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards us for spending more of your money, which is the wrong incentive. We work on a flat monthly fee agreed against the scope of channels.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do, always. We work inside your accounts under your billing. If we stop working together you keep every account, every audience and the full history — there is nothing to hand back because it was never ours.

How soon do results show?

Tracking fixes show immediately because the reporting starts telling the truth. Media performance depends on your sales cycle and budget: shorter-cycle D2C usually reads within weeks, considered B2B purchases take a quarter or more before the numbers mean anything.
