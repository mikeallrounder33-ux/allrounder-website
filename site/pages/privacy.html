Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, who else can see it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated7 September 2026
Applies toThis website

Who we are. This site is operated by [Registered business name], trading as Allrounder Marketing (“we”, “us”), [business address], Canada. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights below, email mikeallrounder33@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the enquiry form or email us, we receive what you choose to send: your name, email address, company name, the engagement type you selected, and the message you write. Nothing on this site asks for payment details, identity documents or any special category of personal information — please don't send those through the form.

Information collected automatically

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Two things still happen automatically, as on any website:

  • Server logs. Our web host records standard request information — IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps — for security and to keep the site running. This is handled by our hosting provider, [hosting provider].
  • Web fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. We receive nothing from this and cannot identify you through it. See Google's privacy policy.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss possible work — on the basis of your consent in sending it, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.
  • To deliver work if you become a client — on the basis of performing our contract with you.
  • To keep the site secure and available — on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating it safely.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations — where the law requires us to keep records.

We do not sell your information, rent it, or use it to build advertising profiles. We won't add you to a marketing list because you sent an enquiry.

Who else sees it

We share personal information only with the service providers that make this site work:

  • FormSubmit — processes the enquiry form and forwards it to our inbox. Form contents pass through their servers, which are located outside Canada. See formsubmit.co.
  • Our email provider ([email provider]) — where your message is delivered and stored.
  • Our hosting provider ([hosting provider]) — which serves this site and keeps the server logs described above.
  • Google Fonts — as described above.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or where necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside Canada, including in the United States. That means your information may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities in those countries. Where we can choose, we use providers that commit to recognised safeguards for cross-border transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become work — kept for up to two years, then deleted.
  • Client records — kept for the length of the engagement and then as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally six years in Canada).
  • Server logs — retained per our hosting provider's standard period.

Your rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and under the UK/EU GDPR where it applies to you, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete it, where we have no continuing legal or contractual reason to keep it
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is what we relied on
  • Receive it in a portable format, where that right applies

Email mikeallrounder33@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to your local supervisory authority if you're in the UK or EU.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it to reply to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — please don't send confidential material through the contact form.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information — for example by adding analytics or a scheduling tool — we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be noted here.

Contact

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: mikeallrounder33@gmail.com, or write to [business address].

In short

The form sends us your name, email and message so we can reply. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and nothing is sold or shared beyond the providers that deliver the message.
