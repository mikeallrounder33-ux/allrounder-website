Why planning only

Three reasons this is a feature rather than a limitation.

No stake in your spend

Agencies usually charge a percentage of media budget, which quietly rewards them for spending more of your money. We charge a flat fee for the thinking, so the recommendation is the recommendation.

The plan is yours

You keep the document, the accounts and the reasoning. Nothing is held hostage to a retainer, and you can execute it with anyone — including the team you already pay.

Cheaper than finding out the hard way

A few weeks of planning costs less than a quarter of budget spent in the wrong channel, and far less than an agency retainer signed before anyone worked out what should run.
